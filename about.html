Who We Are

Brookview Ridge Schools

Brookview Ridge Schools is a premium private school in Marurui, along the Northern Bypass in Nairobi, offering a nurturing learning journey from Daycare through Grade 1. We exist to give children a gentle, structured and engaging start to their education — one built on care, consistency and genuine attention to every learner.

Guided by our motto, "A legacy of excellence," we bring together Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum with a warm, well-resourced environment where teachers, parents and children work as one team toward every child's growth.

Our Philosophy

Learning Rooted in Care

We believe young children learn best when they feel safe, seen and free to explore. Our philosophy blends structured academic learning with purposeful play — because knowledge and confidence grow best together, not apart.

Every classroom, activity and routine at Brookview Ridge is designed with a simple standard in mind: would we be comfortable if this were our own child?

Our Vision

To be a trusted home of early learning where every child is nurtured to discover their potential and grow with confidence.

Our Mission

To provide a safe, structured and engaging learning environment that builds strong academic, social and personal foundations for every child in our care.

What We Believe About Children

Every Child Deserves to Be Seen

We believe every child is naturally curious, capable and full of potential. Our role is not to rush that potential, but to nurture it — giving each learner room to explore, make mistakes, ask questions and grow at a pace that is right for them.

Our Commitment to Parents

You Are Part of the Journey

We see education as a partnership. We commit to open communication, a safe and caring environment, and a genuine investment in your child's growth — so that you always feel informed, involved and confident in your choice of school.
