Our Educational Philosophy

Structured Learning, Delivered Through Play

Brookview Ridge Schools follows Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), which focuses on building practical, real-world competencies rather than rote memorisation. We deliver this curriculum through early childhood development best practice — meaning young children learn primarily through guided play, hands-on activity and social interaction.

As children progress from our early years programmes into PP1, PP2 and Grade 1, learning becomes gradually more structured, while still remaining engaging, supportive and appropriate to each child's stage of development.

What Our Curriculum Builds

Competencies That Last a Lifetime

Every learning area is chosen to build real, transferable skills — not just short-term test readiness.

Literacy

Early language, reading and communication skills.

Numeracy

Number sense, counting and early mathematical thinking.

Communication

Confident self-expression, listening and language skills.

Creativity

Art, music and imaginative, open-ended activities.

Social Development

Sharing, empathy, teamwork and healthy relationships.

Motor Skills

Fine and gross motor development through hands-on play.

School Readiness

The habits, focus and confidence needed for Grade 1 and beyond.

Confidence & Independence

Self-belief and the ability to try, fail and try again.

Personalized Learning

Every Child Learns at Their Own Pace

No two children develop in exactly the same way. Our small class sizes allow teachers to notice how each learner is progressing and adjust their guidance accordingly — offering more support where it's needed, and more challenge where a child is ready for it.

Language Enrichment

Free German Language Exposure

Alongside their core CBC learning, children at Brookview Ridge are introduced to the German language as part of our enrichment programme — giving them early exposure to a new language in a fun, age-appropriate way.
