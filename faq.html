Admissions

How do I apply for admission?

Our admissions process is simple and personal, in five steps: enquire using the form, WhatsApp, phone or email; our team gets in touch to understand your child's needs; you book a visit to tour the campus and meet our teachers; you explore our classrooms and daily routines in person; then we finalise enrolment and welcome your child to Brookview Ridge.

Start your enquiry on the Admissions page →

Is admission currently open?

Admissions are generally ongoing. Current openings and any term-specific offers are confirmed on our Admissions page or by contacting the school office directly.

What are your fees?

Fees are confirmed directly with our school office so we can guide you accurately for your child's programme. Please contact us or reach us on WhatsApp for current fee information.

Can I book a visit before applying?

Yes — in fact we encourage it. Touring our campus, seeing our classrooms and meeting our team in person is part of our admissions process. Book a visit here →

Programs & Curriculum

What curriculum do you follow?

We follow Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), which focuses on building practical, real-world competencies rather than rote memorisation. We deliver it through early childhood development best practice, meaning young children learn primarily through guided play, hands-on activity and social interaction, becoming gradually more structured as children move into PP1, PP2 and Grade 1.

What programs do you offer?

Daycare, Playgroup, PP1, PP2 and Grade 1 — a single continuous learning journey from a child's earliest days away from home through to the start of formal primary school. See all our programs →

Do you teach any languages besides English?

Yes. Alongside core CBC learning, children at Brookview Ridge are introduced to German as part of our language enrichment programme, in a fun, age-appropriate way.

How big are your classes?

We keep class sizes small by design, so every learner gets real attention and teachers can adjust their guidance to each child's pace and personality rather than teaching to a crowd.
