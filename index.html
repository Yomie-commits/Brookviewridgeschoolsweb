Our Approach

Learning Should Build More Than Knowledge.

At Brookview Ridge, learning is hands-on and playful, but never accidental. Every activity is chosen to build a specific part of a child's development.

  • Learning through play, exploration and creativity
  • Social development, communication and teamwork
  • Confidence, independence and school readiness
  • Motor development through hands-on activities
  • Strong academic foundations aligned to the CBC
