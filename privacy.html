Who we are

Brookview Ridge Schools, Marurui, Northern Bypass, Nairobi, Kenya ("the school", "we"). We are responsible for the personal information described here. Contact: brookview.marurui@gmail.com, 0723 384 746. [Name and contact of the school's data protection lead, to be added.]

What we collect on this website

  • Enquiry and visit forms (Contact, Admissions, Transport): your name, phone number, email address, your child's name and age or class, the programme or area you ask about, a preferred visit date and your message. We also record the date and time you accepted this notice, your IP address and browser type (to prevent spam and abuse).
  • Optional follow-up choices: whether you are happy to be followed up by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Analytics (only if you choose "Accept" on the analytics banner): anonymous information about how pages are used.

Why we use it and our basis

We use enquiry details to reply to you, arrange visits and guide you through admission. Our basis is your consent (the box you tick when you send a form) and our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing. We send SMS or WhatsApp messages only if you agreed to them, and you can withdraw at any time (reply STOP to WhatsApp, or tell us).

Children's information

You may give us a child's name and age so we can advise on admission. Please share only what is needed. Information about enrolled pupils is handled separately in the school's records under the school's own policies.

Who handles it for us

Information is stored on the school's system. Service providers process it on our behalf only to deliver the service: our hosting and email providers, and, where enabled, Cloudflare Turnstile (spam protection), Google Analytics (only after you accept analytics), Google Maps (only when you click "Show map") and Cloudinary (public news photos; never your form data). Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you may ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to or restrict how we use it, or withdraw consent. Contact us using the details above. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke).

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If analytics is switched on, a banner asks you to accept or decline; nothing is sent to Google until you accept, and you can change your choice at any time using "Analytics settings" in the footer.
